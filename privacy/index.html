The short version

We collect only what we need to make and deliver your video. We use the details you share about the person your video is for only to make that video, and we delete them within 60 days of delivery. We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who we are

Santa Magic is an Australian business based in Melbourne, Victoria ("we", "us"). This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit santamagic.com.au, buy a video or use the Santa Magic Studio.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

About you

  • your name and email address
  • your order details and order code
  • your messages in the Studio chat and any emails you send us

Payments are handled by Stripe. We don't see or store your full card details.

About the person your video is for

  • their first name or nickname, and how to pronounce it
  • their age, if you share it
  • the details you choose to share for the script, such as proud moments, interests, wishes, and the first names of family members or pets

We ask for first names only. Please don't share surnames, addresses, schools, health information or other sensitive details. The Studio won't include them in a script.

From your device

When you visit our website or use the Studio, our providers may collect technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, so the chat and checkout work securely.

Details about children

The Studio is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information directly from children. Details about a child are provided by a parent, guardian or other adult with their permission, and are used only to make that child's video.

How we use it

  • to write your script, create your voice previews and make your video
  • to deliver your video and order code by email
  • to help you if you contact us
  • to prevent fraud and misuse, and to meet our legal and tax obligations

If you agree, we may email you about Santa Magic in future. Every email has an unsubscribe link. We never sell your information, and we never use the details about the person your video is for in marketing.

AI and your details

Santa Magic uses AI services to run the Studio chat, write your script and create the voice and video. The details you share are sent to these services only to produce your video. You review and approve the script before anything is made.

We don't use your details to train AI models. We don't use AI to make decisions about you that have a legal or similarly significant effect.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with the service providers that help us run Santa Magic, and only as needed for them to do so.

How long we keep it

  • Details about the person your video is for, your script and your Studio chat: deleted within 60 days of your video being delivered, so we can help if you have a problem with your video.
  • Your video link: active for 7 days. Please download your video to keep it.
  • Order and payment records: kept for 5 years, as Australian tax law requires.

Our providers may keep limited copies for short periods under their own policies, for example in backups.

Keeping it secure

We use secure, encrypted connections, limit access to the people who need it, and take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we'll tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner where the law requires it.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or the details you shared about the person your video is for. Email support@santamagic.com.au and we'll respond within 30 days.

If you have a privacy concern, please email us first so we can put it right. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Cookies

Our website, Studio chat and checkout use cookies and similar technology that are needed for them to work, such as keeping your chat session open and processing payment securely. We don't use advertising cookies on this website.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.

Questions? Email support@santamagic.com.au.
