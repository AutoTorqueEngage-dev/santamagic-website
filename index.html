Questions

How long does it take?

Most people finish the Studio in 5 to 10 minutes. Once you approve the voice preview, your video is usually ready in about 10 minutes.

Can I make changes?

Yes. You can ask for up to 5 rounds of script changes and hear a voice preview, with up to 2 more previews if you'd like a different voice or a fresh take. Because you approve the script and voice first, your video is final once it's created.

Will Santa promise a present?

No. Santa mentions their wishes warmly without promising anything, so the magic holds whatever Christmas morning brings.

How do I get my video?

It appears in the Studio and is emailed to you. The link stays active for 7 days, so download your video to keep it.

What if something isn't right?

Email support@santamagic.com.au with your order code and our team will help.
