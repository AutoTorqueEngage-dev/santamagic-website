For OllieToy Workshop Santa, Voice 1

Well, hello there, Ollie! It's Santa. I'm sitting at my workbench in the toy workshop, and I've put my tools down especially, because I wanted to have a proper chat with you.

You see, I've been looking over the Christmas list, and your name has a lovely big gold star next to it this year. I heard you finished your very first season of Nippers1. Running into those waves, even on the chilly mornings, takes real courage, Ollie. I'm very proud of you.

And I hear you bowled Pop out in the backyard cricket2! Clean through the stumps. I'd say Pop is still talking about it.

You've been a wonderful big brother to Ruby3, too. Sharing your dinosaur books4 with her and showing her how to build a proper sandcastle. That's exactly the kind of kindness that makes the elves smile.

Now, one of my elves, Hazel, has been reading about dinosaurs on her lunch break, and she has a hundred questions about the triceratops4. I told her the real expert lives in Australia, and his name is Ollie.

So keep being brave, keep being kind, and enjoy every sunny day of the holidays. Merry Christmas, Ollie, from me and everyone here in the workshop.

Questions

How much is it?

A$10.95 for one personalised video, with everything included. There are no extras at checkout.

How long is the video?

Up to 90 seconds, which is long enough to feel personal and short enough to watch again and again.

How long does it take?

The Studio takes about 5 to 10 minutes. Once you approve the voice preview, your video is delivered by email within 15 minutes.

Can I get one video for two children?

Each video is made for one child, so Santa can speak to them personally. Ordering for brothers, sisters or cousins? Use the same email for each order and every video is written fresh, so no two sound alike.

Is Santa Magic made with AI?

Yes. Santa and Mrs Claus are brought to life with AI video and voice technology. The script is written with you in the Studio, you approve every word, and you hear the voice before the video is made.

Can I make changes?

Yes. You can ask for up to 5 rounds of script changes and hear a voice preview, with up to 2 more previews if you'd like a different voice. Because you approve the script and voice first, your video is final once it's made.

Will Santa promise a present?

No. Santa mentions their wish warmly without promising anything, so the magic holds whatever Christmas morning brings.

How do I get my video?

It is emailed to you within 15 minutes as a link to an MP4 video that plays on phones, tablets, computers and smart TVs. The link stays active for 7 days, so download your video to keep it.

What happens to the details I share?

We use them only to write and make your video. We never sell them or use them for advertising, and we delete the details about the person your video is for within 60 days of delivery. Our privacy policy has the full detail.

Can I get a refund if I change my mind?

Yes, if you haven't approved a script yet, you can ask for a full refund within 30 days of ordering. Once you approve your script, change of mind refunds no longer apply, because your video is made from the script and voice you chose.

What if something isn't right?

Email support@santamagic.com.au with your order code. If something went wrong on our side, we'll remake your video or refund you. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law always apply. See our Terms.
